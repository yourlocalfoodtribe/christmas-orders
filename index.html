❓ Quick answers
How far do you deliver?
We deliver to Nundah, Wavell Heights, Chermside, Boondall, Virginia, Geebung, Zillmere, Taigum, Fitzgibbon, Bracken Ridge, Deagon, Sandgate, Shorncliffe, Brighton, Aspley and Carseldine. Not on the list? Send us a message and we will see what we can do.
How does everything arrive?
Everything arrives Frozen or Refrigerated, in its original sealed packaging. Into the fridge when it reaches you - it keeps 5-7 days there. Take cakes out about an hour before serving. Not eating it yet? It lasts 6 months in the freezer.
Can I pick up instead?
Yes - free pickup from Taigum Square. Type PICKUP in the address box at checkout and we will send you the pickup details.
How do I pay?
PayID (yourfoodtribe@gmail.com) is free, or pay by card or PayPal at checkout.
What if something is not right?
Message us straight away and we will sort it out.
